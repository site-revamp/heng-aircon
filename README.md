# Heng Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Heng Aircon Pte. Ltd. provides aircon servicing, repairs, chemical cleaning, installation, and replacement for homes and businesses in Singapore. The proposed redesign helps visitors find the business, compare service options, and book by phone or WhatsApp.

## Main Improvements

1. **Correct the map and directions.** The contact page appears to show the London Eye. The redesign would replace that embed with the confirmed Singapore location, display the full business address consistently, and add a direct Google Maps directions link.
2. **Clarify service prices and conditions.** Confirm whether the one- and two-unit yearly contracts are both S$200. The pricing section would explain how chemical wash and overhaul differ, show how gas top-up rates vary by refrigerant and scope, and highlight the S$60 diagnostic fee and its waiver when customers proceed with the repair.
3. **Show evidence of service quality and simplify booking.** Give verifiable reviews, genuine technician and project photos, and confirmed workmanship and repair warranty terms more visibility. Clarify the basis of the advertised 10+ years of industry experience alongside the company’s 2020 incorporation date. Make phone and WhatsApp booking actions easy to find.

## Design Improvements

A consistent pricing layout would bring rates and conditions together. A dedicated contact section would group the address, map, and directions link, with clear booking actions near service information.
